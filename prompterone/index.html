Prompter One

A teleprompter that scrolls your script over the camera while you record. Pay once or subscribe – both unlock the same features.

Questions

  • The text is too fast or too slow. Speed, size, colors and mirroring are saved per script. Open the script and adjust them before recording.
  • Where are my recordings? In your Photos library, after you tap Save. Prompter One only asks for add-only access.
  • I bought it on another device. Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • How do I cancel the yearly plan? Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your iPhone. The one-time unlock never renews.

日本語
